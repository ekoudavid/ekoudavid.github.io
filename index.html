About me

Evidence people can trust, from the field to the boardroom.

I started out entering patient records in HIV clinics in Soroti and Kigezi, where a missing field could mean a missed follow-up. That taught me that data quality is a duty of care, not admin.

Today I run MEAL work at LEAD4africa: tracer studies, KAP surveys, endline evaluations and the dashboards that carry their findings to managers, boards and donors.

Alongside that, I'm studying AI and data science and building tools that make this work faster and more local, because I believe African programmes should help shape how AI is used in development.

Monitoring, evaluation & learning

MEAL case studies

Selected assignments from youth development and health programmes: the question, the method, my role and what the evidence changed.

Tracer study

Tracking alumni outcomes years after the programme

Question
Where are former participants now in education, work, enterprise and leadership?
Method
Alumni tracer survey with a control-group comparison, structured by the programme's four pillars: Leadership, Entrepreneurship, Academics and Discipleship.
My role
Data quality assurance in Salesforce, sex-disaggregated analysis, donor-facing dashboard.
Result
A reporting-ready alumni dataset and outcome evidence used in board, QBR and donor reporting.
KAP survey

Knowledge, attitudes & practices in a youth sport programme

Question
Did participants' knowledge, attitudes and practices shift, and did change differ for young women and men?
Method
Quantitative KAP survey plus qualitative data collection, gender-disaggregated throughout.
My role
Analysis in R, interactive Shiny and Power BI dashboards, annual report inputs.
Result
Findings the team could explore by sex and site, feeding the programme's annual report.
Endline evaluation

Senior Six endline: what changed for students

Question
What changes in leadership, values and character did students experience by the end of school?
Method
Mixed methods: focus group discussions and key informant interviews alongside survey data.
My role
Conducted focus groups and key informant interviews, synthesised findings and drafted donor reporting.
Result
Donor-ready evidence on outcomes that are hard to measure with numbers alone.

Live app

Life Expectancy Explorer

A machine-learning model that predicts country life expectancy from WHO health, economic and schooling indicators, deployed as an interactive Streamlit app. The main model deliberately leaves out mortality indicators; a second version that includes them shows how target leakage inflates accuracy. It's the same discipline I apply when judging whether an indicator really measures an outcome.
