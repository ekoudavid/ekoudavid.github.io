    Monitoring, evaluation & learning

    Three studies, three questions

    Alumni tracer study

    Where are our graduates now?

    A tracer survey with a control-group comparison, following former participants into education, work, enterprise and leadership. Findings are organised by the programme's four pillars: Leadership, Entrepreneurship, Academics and Discipleship.

    My part: data quality assurance in Salesforce, sex-disaggregated analysis, and the donor-facing dashboard used in board and QBR reporting.

    KAP survey

    Did change differ for young women and men?

    A knowledge, attitudes and practices survey in a youth sport-for-development programme, with qualitative follow-up and every result disaggregated by sex.

    My part: analysis in R, interactive Shiny and Power BI dashboards the team explores by sex and site, and inputs to the annual report.

    Senior Six endline evaluation

    What can't a questionnaire capture?

    A mixed-methods endline on leadership, values and character among students finishing school, where numbers alone miss most of the story.

    My part: focus group discussions and key informant interviews, synthesis, and donor reporting.

    Programme and donor details are anonymised. No beneficiary data appears on this site.

    AI, data science & machine learning

    What I'm building

    Live app

    Life Expectancy Explorer

    A model that predicts a country's life expectancy from WHO health, economic and schooling indicators, deployed as a Streamlit app anyone can use.

    Open the app Read the code

    Why the main model ignores mortality data

    Adult and infant mortality almost spell out life expectancy, so a model fed them looks brilliant and teaches nothing. I kept them out, and built a second model with them in to show the gap. It's the same test I apply to any indicator: does it measure the outcome, or just repeat it?
